    How to Play

    Hear the beat
    Guess the tempo

    A round, in three beats

    1. 01

      Listen

      A metronome starts ticking. The range hint shows where the tempo lives, and it narrows every round.

    2. 02 120bpm

      Guess

      Type your BPM and hit Enter. The clock is running from the first tick.

    3. 03 SAB

      Score

      See how close you were, then go again. Three rounds make a game.

    What earns points

    Accuracy 100 pts

    −20exact+20

    Every BPM you're off costs 5 points. 20 or more off and the round scores zero.

    Speed bonus +33 pts

    0s10s

    Commit fast. The bonus fades to nothing by 10 seconds.

    Time penalty −0.5 / sec

    0s2s10s

    After 2 seconds the clock starts taking points back.
